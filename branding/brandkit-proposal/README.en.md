# Grassfield — proposed brand kit

English translation of [README.md](README.md), the owner's French text, which remains the reference.

**Status: identity under validation.** This identity takes up the first generated concept and the retouch of it chosen during the conversation. The SVG symbol is an interpretive vector redraw: it differs slightly from the generated image. It is integrated into the system logo resources; the other elements remain proposals. Visual and legal validation are still to be done before a public launch or a filing.

Open [the brand board](brandboard.html) in a browser. The [source concept](../concepts/grassfields-original-refined/logo.png) and its [prompt](../concepts/grassfields-original-refined/PROMPT.md) are kept. The SVGs and their transparent PNG exports are in [assets](assets/).

## Idea

A rising silhouette and layered curves evoke the momentum and the reliefs of the Grassfields of West Cameroon. The official name of the current offer is **Grassfield Desktop**; **Grassfield OS** names the ISO and its upcoming installer. The signature "Grassfield" works on its own when the context is clear. Avoid giving the symbol a specific traditional cultural meaning that has not been verified.

## Logo

- `symbol-black`, `symbol-white`, `symbol-green`: the symbol alone, respectively for light backgrounds, dark backgrounds and brand uses on light backgrounds.
- `lockup-horizontal-black/white`: the main signature with the name, for the website, documents and communications.
- `lockup-stacked-black/white`: the centred signature, for vertical compositions.
- Each variant is provided as an editable SVG and as a transparent PNG exported 1,024 px wide. The SVG signatures use Inter text: install Inter or convert the text to outlines before sending to a printer. The PNG keeps the local rendering.

**Proposed clear space:** keep free space around the symbol of at least 20 % of its height. For a signature, apply the same margin around the whole. Do not distort, slant or frame the symbol, and do not add a shadow, an outline or a gradient to it. On a photo, place the logo in a calm area with enough contrast; otherwise use a plain surface.

**Sizes:** for communication, start at a symbol height of 32 px and a horizontal signature width of 200 px. These are provisional guides: the detail at the bottom may disappear at small sizes. Check on screen and in print. Do not scale down the PNG instead of starting from the SVG.

**Optical version (16–24 px):** the interface uses two files on the 24-unit grid. `branding/icons/symbolic/grassfield-logo-full.svg` is the full symbol, unchanged, for 32 px and more: the source for large uses (the website, future large About surfaces); no system surface shows it today. `branding/icons/symbolic/grassfield-logo.svg` is its optical version for 16–24 px (menu bar, About window, Settings, Search): the same rising blades over the same rounded base, with fewer and wider white gaps. The thin slit between the tall blade and the right blade closes into a V where it would blur, the left notch is more open, the base keeps its wave outline without the inner slit, and a single gap of about 1.4 px at 16 px separates the blades from the base. The drawing of the full symbol does not change. This optical version follows the symbol's status: its final adoption and its legal validation remain the owner's.

## Colours

| Role | Colour | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#101915` | Text, symbol on light backgrounds |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Light background, symbol on dark backgrounds |
| Grassfield green | `#26763F` | Main accent, logo on light backgrounds; the product's existing light accent |
| Light green | `#4FB36B` | Accent on dark backgrounds; the product's existing dark accent |
| Light surface | `#F4F7F2` | Proposed communication background |
| Forest | `#173C2B` | Proposed communication background |

Computed contrasts: ink/white **17.92:1**, Grassfield green/white **5.61:1**, light green/ink **6.82:1**. Check every other combination and the real states of the interface; do not convey information by colour alone. The colours proposed for communication do not change the application's tokens.

## Typography

**Inter**: headings in SemiBold, text in Regular or Medium. Fallback font: the system sans-serif; the project uses Liberation Sans if Inter is not installed. Keep a simple hierarchy: heading 48/52, subheading 24/30, body 16/24 px as a web starting point. The provided signature uses Inter SemiBold; its SVG display depends on the installed font. The font is not distributed in this kit: check its licence and the version used before distribution.

## Tone and applications

A clear, calm and welcoming voice: short sentences, concrete benefits, accessible vocabulary in French as in English. Name the product "Grassfield Desktop" in first presentations. For materials: sober surfaces, plenty of space and a measured emphasis on green; mountain photographs can serve as context without hurting legibility. The board shows application ideas, not captures of the real interface.
